## Decision summary

`devgod` should ship a mandatory frontend art-direction phase for substantive redesign work and use public/free inspiration sources that are broad, curated, and structured enough for autonomous browsing.

## Approved conditions

- required sources must stay public/free
- the workflow must require actual references and multiple directions, not only the names of inspiration galleries
- the first slice should document runner-ups and exclusions so future maintainers understand why some popular sites were not made mandatory

## Dissent

The main risk is turning frontend work into ceremony without improving output. To mitigate that, the workflow should require a small, concrete reference pack and should tie screenshot critique back to visible hierarchy problems rather than performative documentation.
