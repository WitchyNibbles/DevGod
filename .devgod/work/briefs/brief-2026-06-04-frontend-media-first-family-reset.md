# Brief

## Task ID

`2026-06-04-frontend-media-first-family-reset`

## Request

Diagnose why `devgod` still produces generic or broken “same design reordered” frontends, research the best current evidence for immersive and media-rich agentic UI generation, upgrade the shipped frontend workflow accordingly, and prove it on a truly different `hexchange` remake.

## Intended outcome

- a source-backed diagnosis of why the current workflow still collapses into dark generic shells with minor garnish
- shipped `devgod` workflow changes that force media-first concept decisions, richer authored assets, and stronger family-reset gates for autonomous frontend agents
- a fresh `hexchange` remake that is visibly from a different family and no longer depends on the old panel-shell primitives
- a scoped audit of `../archon` that captures any Codex-facing frontend workflow behavior worth porting into the allowed `devgod` control-layer files

## Primary user

`devgod` maintainers and autonomous frontend agents; downstream beneficiary is the end-user of generated frontends.

## Constraints and non-goals

- no preservation constraints beyond functionality and critical control discoverability
- no requirement to use 3D or generated images on every task, but the workflow must force an explicit decision and rationale instead of silently defaulting to CSS-only styling
- avoid generic, broken, panel-first results and barely noticeable ambient motion masquerading as innovation

## Acceptance criteria

- research explains why the current workflow still reuses the same design primitives and underuses generated/authored media
- the shipped workflow requires a media-first concept choice and rejects broad remakes that only reorder known shell patterns
- the shipped workflow treats vibe images as style language and interaction personality, not pasted art, while still allowing generated assets or 3D when the concept calls for them
- `hexchange` receives a fresh remake that proves a different design family, preserves working controls, and passes focused verification

## Facts

- the user explicitly wants this aimed at `devgod`, not only `hexchange`
- the user explicitly asked for an audit of `../archon` and a detailed backlog for what `devgod` should adapt
- the user explicitly rejected the latest `hexchange` result as broken and still “the same design but reordered”
- the user explicitly says the issue applies to future `devgod` frontend development in general
- the user explicitly called out that image generation features and 3D techniques are not being used, or even considered, in the current workflow
- the current proof screenshots still show dark shells, oversized serif headings, familiar card/panel primitives, and garnish-level motion

## Assumptions

- the user wants end-to-end execution, not planning only
- the strongest fix will involve both source-backed workflow changes and a new blank-slate proof pass in `hexchange`
- generated imagery, authored illustration, Rive-class motion, or 3D/WebGL are tools to choose deliberately, not mandatory garnish for every screen

## Risks

- stronger media-first guidance could devolve into gratuitous motion or heavy rendering unless reduced-motion and technical-fit checks stay strong
- forcing more asset craft could encourage fake “cute placeholders” unless semantic justification remains part of the gate
- a more radical remake could again hide or remove critical trading controls if functionality-preservation checks are not kept visible throughout implementation

## Bounded investigation budget

- 6 source-backed web research threads across official docs and primary product guidance
- 5 shallow local inspections of current frontend workflow surfaces and the latest `hexchange` evidence before implementation changes

## Stop/go

Go. Clarification is sufficient; proceed through research, workflow changes, and a fresh remake proof.
